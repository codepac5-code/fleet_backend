DriverX · Driver app

A driver app built for real office operations.

DriverX gives Qatar drivers one guided flow — availability, trip requests, navigation, earnings and safety — connected to your office. Less confusion on the road, more completed trips, clear QAR earnings.

Create Cloud Office View driver workflow
Availability control

Online · Busy · Offline

Drivers decide when they work with one clear status switch. Your office sees availability live, so dispatch always knows who can take the next trip.

  • One-tap Online / Busy / Offline
  • Live demand & eligible service classes
  • Visible to your office in real time
  • Break, fuel, prayer & vehicle reasons

Earnings clarity

Drivers see exactly what they earn — in QAR

DriverX makes money transparent: gross earnings, cash collected, digital earnings, office fees and net expected payout — per trip and per day, with acceptance and completion performance.

  • Gross, cash collected & digital earnings
  • Office fees & net expected payout
  • Acceptance & completion rates
  • Today, weekly & monthly views
