FleetOS Cloud Office

Your taxi office — in the cloud.

FleetOS Cloud Office is the flagship system that turns a traditional taxi office into a connected digital operation: bookings, documents, drivers, dispatch, payments, commission, support and reports, all in one place.

Everything your office runs on

One cloud system, fully connected

Bookings

Receive digital bookings from Fleet Ride and your own channels, all in one queue.

Dispatch

Assign trips automatically or manually and watch them move through the lifecycle.

Drivers & vehicles

Onboard drivers and vehicles, verify documents and track performance.

Payments

Track payments by method and status, with wallet and settlement records.

Commission

Control per-vehicle commission and three-way splits, recorded in QAR.

Documents

Upload and manage your commercial registration, licenses and brand assets.

Support

Resolve issues with a structured ticket workflow and FleetOS escalation.

Reports

Daily, weekly and monthly analytics, exportable to Excel and CSV.
