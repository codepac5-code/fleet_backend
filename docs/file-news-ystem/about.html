About FleetOS

Operator-first mobility, built in Qatar.

FleetOS for Technology & Software L.L.C. is a cloud taxi office and mobility platform incubated at Qatar Science & Technology Park, built to digitise Qatar's regulated operators in line with Qatar National Vision 2030.

Our model

Strengthen operators — don't replace them

Global ride-hailing apps took pricing, demand and customers away from local offices. FleetOS gives that control back: a cloud operating system that lets licensed Qatar operators compete digitally while keeping their brand, drivers and customers.

  • Operator-first, not a ride-hailing replacement
  • A cloud taxi office system & marketplace
  • Built for Qatar's regulated operators
  • Aligned with Qatar National Vision 2030
