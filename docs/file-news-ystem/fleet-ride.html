Fleet Ride · Rider app

Let riders choose your office, not just a car.

Fleet Ride is the rider marketplace where customers in Qatar compare and choose trusted, licensed taxi offices on price, rating, ETA and support — then book the one they trust. Your office keeps the customer.

The office marketplace

Riders compare trusted offices — and pick yours

Instead of a faceless assignment, riders see real licensed offices and choose.

1 · Choose a ride type
City, Premium, Airport, Travel & more — in QAR.

2 · Compare taxi offices
By fastest, best price or highest rated.

3 · Confirm your ride
Selected office and QAR fare, confirmed.

Trip experience

Live tracking and a clear QAR fare

Riders follow their trip in real time with a live meter for Ride and Premium, or a fixed A-to-Z fare for Travel — always shown up front, always in QAR.

  • Live trip tracking from pickup to drop-off
  • Live meter for Ride & Premium
  • Fixed A-to-Z fare for Travel
  • Driver & office details on every trip
Support & rating

Two-layer support, separate ratings

Your office handles the trip first-line while FleetOS provides safety oversight and escalation. After the ride, riders rate the driver and the office separately — keeping quality and trust high.

  • Office support + FleetOS escalation
  • In-app safety, sharing & SOS
  • Digital QAR receipt after every trip
  • Separate driver & office ratings

App access during operator onboarding

The Fleet Ride app for your riders becomes available as part of your FleetOS cloud office onboarding.
